Privacy & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

How your conversations remain strictly confidential.

End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

Messages are encrypted directly in your browser using AES-GCM 256-bit encryption before transmission. The decryption key is contained entirely within the URL fragment (after the # symbol), which browsers never send to servers. The server only relays ciphertexts and has zero ability to read your messages.

No Accounts & Zero Logs

We do not require usernames, emails, or passwords. Each visitor is assigned a temporary cryptographic session ID. Message history is transient and evaporates when rooms expire or participants disconnect.

Threat Model & Limitations

While transport and storage are mathematically secured, absolute privacy depends on operational security: anyone with whom you share the invitation link can enter the room. Ensure your device is free of malware or malicious browser extensions capable of keylogging or screen capturing.
